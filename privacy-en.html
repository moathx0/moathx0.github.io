Privacy Policy

Application: Shikaku
Developer: Moath Saad Nasser Alkwaikebi
Effective Date: June 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Shikaku, a logic puzzle game inspired by the traditional Japanese game. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our game.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information about you. The game does not request your real name, email address, phone number, location, or any other identifying data.

All data the game stores is kept locally on your device only (in the browser's localStorage) and is never transmitted to any server. This includes:

  • The nickname you choose within the game
  • Your selected avatar
  • Your level progress, stars, and player level
  • Coins, hearts, and inventory items
  • Game settings (theme, sound, difficulty mode)
  • Achievements and completed missions

This data is accessible only to you. You can delete it entirely at any time by uninstalling the game or by pressing the "Reset Progress" button inside the settings.

3. Third-Party Services (Advertising)

The game may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. When these ads are shown, Google may collect information such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device type and operating system
  • Approximate IP address
  • Your interactions with ads

This data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, which you can review here:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You may reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings at any time.

4. Children's Privacy

The game is rated Everyone and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children under the age of 13, and we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Google AdMob ads displayed in the game will be of the Non-Personalized type when the game is played by users believed to be children, in accordance with Google's policies.

5. Permissions Requested

The game may request the following permissions:

  • Internet: Used only to display ads — the game itself works offline.
  • Vibration: For tactile feedback on wins and errors (can be disabled in game settings).

6. Data Security

Since all game data is stored locally on your device and is never sent to any external server, there are no leakage risks from our side. The security of your data depends on the security of your personal device.

We recommend not sharing your device with untrusted persons if you wish to preserve your game progress.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When substantive changes occur, we will publish the updated version at the same URL and update the "Effective Date" above. We recommend reviewing this page periodically.

8. Governing Law

This policy is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Any disputes arising from the use of this application are subject to the jurisdiction of Saudi courts.
